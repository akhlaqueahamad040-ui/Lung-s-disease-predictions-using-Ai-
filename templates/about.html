About LungAI

AI-powered lung disease prediction system

Our Mission

LungAI is dedicated to providing accessible, AI-powered lung disease risk assessment and analysis tools. We combine machine learning with medical imaging analysis to help users understand their respiratory health.

Features

  • Symptom-based risk prediction using trained ML models
  • X-ray image analysis with AI-powered radiological assessment
  • Personalized health history and tracking
  • Secure user accounts and data privacy
  • Clinical recommendations and guidance
